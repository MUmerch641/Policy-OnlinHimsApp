Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2025

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information in our mobile application ("App"). By using this App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal and health-related information:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth and age
  • Gender
  • Guardian's name
  • Phone number
  • CNIC (National ID)
  • Health ID
  • City
  • Reference source
  • HIMS Number (Hospital Internal ID)
  • Hospital name

2. How We Use Your Information

We use this data solely for the following purposes:

  • Managing patient appointments
  • Searching and verifying patient records
  • Improving patient experience in the hospital system

3. Data Storage and Security

All collected data is stored securely. We use encrypted HTTPS connections to transmit data and limit access to authorized medical staff only. We do not store sensitive information on the device or share it with third-party services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share or sell your personal data to any third-party organizations. Your information is used only within the hospital network where the appointment is made.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request deletion of your personal data. To do so, please contact us at: hr@curelogics.org

6. Children’s Privacy

This app is intended for use by patients and guardians. Children under 13 should only use the app under adult supervision.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in this document with a new effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: hr@curelogics.org
